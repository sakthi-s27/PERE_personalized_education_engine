# PERE — Student Guide & Judge Talking Points

## Student mode: what do I do?

1. **Pick a problem** — Start with the Fraction Addition challenge.
2. **Choose a student reasoning path** — Student A, B, or C demonstrates that the same answer can come from different mental models.
3. **Trigger AI diagnosis** — Watch the reasoning telemetry identify the error pattern, misconception, root cause, and confidence.
4. **Inspect the 3D map** — Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover a node for the cognitive stage, and click a node for the explanation.
5. **Read the Cognitive Fingerprint** — Identify the student's weakest skill and current mastery signals.
6. **Follow the Learning Path** — Complete the targeted intervention selected for that misconception.
7. **Try Adaptive Practice** — Correct answers move difficulty up; wrong answers trigger targeted remediation.
8. **Check Before vs After** — Confirm whether mastery and accuracy improved after intervention.

## What to demonstrate in a 3-minute judge demo

- Start with **SAME ANSWER. DIFFERENT MIND.**
- Show Student A, B, and C all producing **3/6**.
- Click **Trigger AI Re-Analysis** and show evidence + confidence without exposing private chain-of-thought.
- Rotate the 3D cognitive map and click the **Root Cause** and **Intervention** nodes.
- Show the student-specific Cognitive Fingerprint and Learning Path.
- Answer the adaptive practice question and point out the difficulty adjustment.
- Finish with Before vs After and the line:

> **Most AI tutors evaluate answers. PERE evaluates understanding.**

## Judge points

### 1. Problem
Traditional answer-based tutoring can tell whether a response is right or wrong, but a wrong response does not uniquely reveal *why* the student is wrong.

### 2. Innovation
PERE adds a reasoning-aware layer:

**Answer → Reasoning → Misconception → Root Cause → Intervention → Practice → Re-evaluation**

### 3. Core differentiator
Three students can give the same wrong answer but receive different diagnoses and different learning paths.

### 4. Explainability
PERE shows concise detected evidence and a confidence score instead of exposing hidden chain-of-thought.

### 5. Personalization
The Cognitive Fingerprint and student signal panel show which skills need attention and why the next learning step was selected.

### 6. 3D visualization
The 3D map is data-driven. Each student changes the concept-to-root-cause path, and the graph is interactive rather than a static decoration.

### 7. Adaptive learning
Practice difficulty changes based on performance, and incorrect answers can route the learner back to targeted remediation.

### 8. Closed loop
PERE does not stop at diagnosis. It re-evaluates the learner after intervention and measures whether mastery improved.

### 9. Teacher value
The teacher heatmap surfaces emerging misconception patterns across groups so interventions can be targeted at class level.

### 10. Measurable outcome
The Before vs After panel communicates the intended product outcome: not merely recommendation, but measurable learning recovery.

## One-line pitch

**PERE is a reasoning-aware education engine that diagnoses the misconception behind a student's answer and adapts the next learning experience to fix the root cause.**
